Before you order

Good to know

How long does a custom set take?

Custom sets take 7–14 business days to make. Then they ship to the US and Canada, usually arriving in 5–12 business days. We'll confirm the timing with you by DM.

What should I send?

A photo, a colour, a mood board or just a vibe, plus your size, XS to XL. Not sure of your size? Send a photo of your bare nails and we'll help.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the design: charms, 3D art and chrome take more time. Shanaya gives you a price by DM before she starts, so there are no surprises.

Will I see the design first?

Yes. Shanaya sketches your set and checks it with you before she paints a single nail.

Can I return a custom set?

Custom sets are made just for you, so they're final sale, unless we made a mistake. See our returns policy.
