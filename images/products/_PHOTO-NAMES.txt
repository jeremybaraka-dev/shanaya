PRODUCT PHOTOS

Each set in js/products.js has three files here, named after its "slug":
  <slug>.webp      main photo (900px wide)       — used on the site
  <slug>-sm.webp   thumbnail (480px wide)         — used on cards, bag, toasts
  <slug>.jpg       JPEG copy (1000px wide)        — used by the Pinterest catalog

Current sets: merlot-garden, downtown-charm, polka-blossom, mocha-chrome,
safari-butterfly, tropic-wild, rose-gold-swirl, scarlet-lily, bow-and-cherry,
berry-leopard, big-apple-eight, orchid-tortoise

To add a new set: drop the original photo in this folder, ask Claude to add it
(it creates the three web sizes and the product entry), then push to GitHub.
Original full-size photos ("now (1).png" etc.) stay on your computer only —
they're kept out of GitHub and the live site because they're ~2.5 MB each.
