Help & policies

Shipping & returns.

Everything to know before you order — written to be fair to you and to us.

Last updated October 22, 2026

Shipping Returns & refunds Cancellations Sizing Wear & care Payments Privacy Contact
01

Shipping

  • Handmade to order. Ready-made sets ship within 3–5 business days. Custom sets take 7–14 business days to make before they ship — we'll confirm timing with you by DM.
  • Delivery time. Once shipped, parcels usually arrive in 5–12 business days in the US and Canada. Times depend on the carrier and your location. You'll get tracking whenever the shipping service provides it.
  • Cost. Orders of $40 or more ship free. Orders under $40 pay a flat $4.99. All prices are in US dollars.
  • Your address. We ship to the address on your PayPal order, so please check it before you pay. Spotted a mistake? Message us within 12 hours and before your order ships, and we'll fix it.
  • Customs & duties. Orders shipped across borders may be charged import duties or taxes by your country. These aren't included in our prices and are paid by the buyer.
  • Lost or late parcels. If tracking hasn't moved for 10 business days, contact us and we'll chase it with the carrier. If it's confirmed lost in transit, we'll send a replacement or refund you. We can't replace parcels that were delivered to the address entered at checkout or marked delivered there.
02

Returns & refunds

  • Hygiene first. Press-ons and stick-ons are personal-care items, so opened, tried-on or worn sets can't be returned.
  • Unopened sets can be returned within 14 days of delivery, unused and in their original packaging. Message us first for the return address. Return shipping is paid by you, and we refund the item price once it arrives and we've checked it. Original shipping isn't refunded.
  • Custom sets are final sale because they're made just for you — unless we made a mistake.
  • Damaged, faulty or wrong item? Message us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the set and packaging. We'll send a free replacement or a full refund including shipping — usually without you having to send anything back.
  • How refunds work. Refunds go back to your original payment method through PayPal, normally within 5–10 business days after we approve them.
03

Cancellations

  • You can cancel within 12 hours of ordering for a full refund, as long as your order hasn't shipped.
  • Custom orders can't be cancelled once we've started making them.
04

Sizing

  • Sets come in sizes XS to XL. Size M fits most hands.
  • Not sure? DM us a photo of your nails next to a ruler before ordering and we'll help you choose.
  • Because of hygiene, opened sets can't be exchanged for a different size — so please check first.
05

Wear & care

  • With good prep, sets can last up to 2 weeks. Wear time varies with nail prep, the adhesive used and your daily activities.
  • If you notice redness, itching or irritation, remove the nails and stop using them. If you have sensitive skin or allergies, check the adhesive before applying.
06

Payments & security

  • Payments are handled securely by PayPal. You can pay with a PayPal account or any debit or credit card — no PayPal account needed.
  • We never see or store your card details.
  • Prices are charged in US dollars. If your card uses another currency, your bank may add a conversion fee.
07

Privacy

  • What we receive: when you order, PayPal shares your name, email and shipping address with us.
  • Why: only to make and ship your order and to contact you about it. We never sell or share your information for marketing.
  • Your bag and wishlist are saved only in your own browser — they aren't sent to us.
  • No ad tracking. This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Services that help run it — PayPal (payments), Vercel (website hosting) and Google Fonts — handle data under their own privacy policies.
  • Want us to delete your details? Just message us.
08

Contact

Questions about an order, sizing or a custom set? DM @nailsbyshanaya_ on TikTok, or reply to your PayPal receipt email. We aim to reply within 1–2 business days.

These policies apply to orders placed on or after the date above. We may update them from time to time; the version shown when you order is the one that applies.
